# Bhoomi Sakha

### Predictive Governance for Early Detection of Land Acquisition Delays

**Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Problem Statement 26017**

> **Bhoomi Sakha** is an AI-powered predictive governance prototype that analyzes the telemetry state of a land acquisition project snapshot and estimates its probability of delay using a trained XGBoost classifier. It extracts model-derived feature contributions to highlight risk-increasing and risk-reducing drivers, providing actionable administrative recommendations so officers can intervene before delays escalate.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

**PS ID:** SIH26017  
**Title:** Predictive Analytics System for Early Detection of Land Acquisition Delays  
**Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development  
**Category:** Software  

Land acquisition is a critical path dependency for national infrastructure corridors (Highways, Railways, Power Transmission, Metro, Industrial SEZs). Delays routinely arise from documentation backlogs, compensation disbursement lags, title disputes, pending statutory approvals, physical possession resistance, inter-agency coordination friction, and schedule slippage under the RFCTLARR Act 2013 framework.

Traditional administrative monitoring is predominantly reactive: an infrastructure project is flagged for review only after critical milestones have elapsed and delay has already cascaded.

### Bhoomi Sakha takes a predictive approach.

Instead of asking:
> *"Why is this project already delayed?"*

the system asks:
> *"Given the project's current snapshot telemetry, what is its statistical probability of experiencing a statutory delay, and which operational vectors are driving that risk?"*

This enables proactive administrative interventions, targeted resource allocation, and prioritized cadastral audits before milestone breaches become irreversible.

# 🎯 What Bhoomi Sakha Does

Given a structured project telemetry snapshot, Bhoomi Sakha computes:

* **Delay Probability:** Continuous probability score ($0.0 \dots 1.0$) generated by the XGBoost binary classifier.
* **Four-Tier Risk Classification:** Mapped into `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, or `CRITICAL` risk tiers.
* **Predicted Outcome:** Binary classification outcome ($\text{Probability} \ge 0.50 \implies \text{Delayed}$).
* **Risk-Increasing Factors:** Top features exerting positive mathematical contribution toward predicted delay.
* **Factors Reducing Predicted Risk:** Top features exerting negative mathematical contribution dampening predicted delay.
* **Feature Contribution Scores:** Exact marginal contribution values extracted from the tree ensemble.
* **Actionable Recommendations:** Contextual administrative directives mapped to primary bottleneck drivers.

# ✨ Key Capabilities

## 1. Predictive Delay Inference

The trained XGBoost model evaluates 76 engineered cadastral, administrative, financial, legal, and operational attributes to calculate delay risk:

```text
Delay Probability: 42.28%
Risk Level: MEDIUM
Predicted Delayed: False
```

> **Note:** The calculated probability is an empirical model risk estimate, not an absolute guarantee of project delay.

---

## 2. Four-Tier Risk Classification

Bhoomi Sakha standardizes risk assessment into four governance tiers:

| Delay Probability | Risk Level | Operational Meaning |
| ----------------: | :--------- | :------------------ |
|          `< 40.0%` | **LOW**    | Normal progress; nominal statutory monitoring. |
|    `40.0% – <60.0%` | **MEDIUM** | Moderate friction; watch-list status for pending milestones. |
|    `60.0% – <80.0%` | **HIGH**   | Significant bottlenecks detected; active intervention required. |
|         `≥ 80.0%` | **CRITICAL** | Severe multi-vector impairment; urgent CALA/Collector escalation. |

The underlying binary decision boundary uses a standard threshold:

$$\text{Probability} \ge 0.50 \implies \text{Predicted Delayed}$$

---

## 3. Model-Based Risk Explanations

Rather than presenting an uninterpretable "black box" percentage, Bhoomi Sakha computes individual feature contributions:

```text
RISK-INCREASING FACTORS
1. Documents Pending
   Value        : 232
   Contribution : +0.4392
   Action       : Prioritize verification of pending land and ownership documents.

2. Active Legal Disputes
   Value        : 8
   Contribution : +0.2814
   Action       : Escalate unresolved legal disputes and identify cases blocking acquisition.
```

---

## 4. Factors Reducing Predicted Risk

The inference engine simultaneously identifies features that dampen predicted delay risk:

```text
FACTORS REDUCING PREDICTED RISK
1. Acquisition Velocity Pct Per 30D
   Value        : 6.13%
   Contribution : -0.3752

2. Schedule Variance Days
   Value        : -7.19 days
   Contribution : -0.1358
```

> **Critical Interpretation:** Model contributions represent statistical influence within the tree ensemble for a specific feature combination. They indicate **predictive influence, not causal proof**.

---

## 5. Actionable Statutory Directives

Identified risk factors are paired with actionable administrative guidance aligned with statutory acquisition procedures:

* **Documentation Backlogs:** Prioritize verification camps for pending ownership records, 7/12 extracts, and genealogical certificates.
* **Compensation Lags:** Expedite Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) escrow reconciliation and CALA award declarations.
* **Litigation & Injunctions:** Refer contested parcel valuations to specialized Lok Adalats or RFCTLARR Authority benches.
* **Inter-Agency Approvals:** Escalate utility shifting (rail, power, forest clearance) to state-level infrastructure coordination committees.

---

## 6. Interactive Scenario Simulation ("What-If" Analysis)

The frontend provides an interactive cockpit where officers can adjust project parameters to observe model sensitivity in real time:

* **Baseline Scenario:** High pending documents, sluggish acquisition velocity, pending legal objections $\implies$ **High / Critical Risk (75–85%)**
* **Mitigated Scenario:** Verification completed, compensation disbursed, disputes settled $\implies$ **Low / Medium Risk (25–38%)**

# 🤖 Machine Learning Model Architecture

The core prediction engine is an **XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting)** binary classifier trained specifically for structured tabular data.

### Model Configuration

* **Algorithm:** XGBoost Classifier (`XGBClassifier`)
* **XGBoost Version:** 3.4.1
* **Objective:** `binary:logistic`
* **Estimators (`n_estimators`):** 500
* **Maximum Tree Depth (`max_depth`):** 8
* **Learning Rate (`learning_rate`):** 0.05
* **Row Subsample Ratio (`subsample`):** 0.85
* **Column Subsample Ratio (`colsample_bytree`):** 0.85
* **Tree Method:** `hist` (Histogram-based gradient boosting)
* **Training Acceleration:** GPU / CUDA (`device="cuda"`)
* **Class Imbalance Handling:** `scale_pos_weight` calculated dynamically from training split
* **Random State:** 42
* **Model Artifact:** `models/baseline_model.json`
* **Feature Schema Artifact:** `models/feature_names.joblib`

# 📊 Dataset & Leakage Prevention

The model was developed using a comprehensive synthetic land-acquisition project snapshot dataset:

```text
Dataset File : data/processed/land_acquisition_3m.parquet
Total Rows   : 3,000,000 snapshots
Projects     : 400,127 unique infrastructure projects
Features     : 76 final features (after encoding)
Delay Rate   : 46.85% (balanced binary distribution)
```

### Strict Data Leakage Prevention Strategy

In longitudinal monitoring data, a single infrastructure project may contain multiple chronological snapshots. Standard random splitting across rows causes **severe data leakage**, as the model memorizes project idiosyncrasies in training and tests on subsequent snapshots of the same project.

To eliminate data leakage, Bhoomi Sakha strictly enforces a **project-level group split**:

* **Splitting Strategy:** `GroupShuffleSplit` (scikit-learn)
* **Grouping Column:** `project_id`
* **Split Ratio:** 80% Train (320,101 projects) / 20% Test (80,026 projects)
* **Project Overlap:** **0 projects** (zero leakage across splits)

### Feature Exclusion (Outcome & Identity Leakage Protection)

The following columns are strictly excluded from the feature matrix prior to training and inference:

1. **Target Variable:** `will_be_delayed` (the binary prediction label)
2. **Outcome-Only Variables:**
   * `additional_delay_days` (actual recorded days of delay; represents outcome information unavailable at snapshot time)
   * `delay_stage` (the statutory stage at which delay occurred)
3. **Identifiers & Timestamps:**
   * `project_id` (identifier used only as the grouping key for splits)
   * `snapshot_date` (specific temporal timestamp)
4. **Geographic Categoricals:**
   * `state` and `district` (currently excluded from model features to prevent geographic overfitting)

# 🔬 Model Training Pipeline

To retrain or verify the model training pipeline:

```bash
python -m src.train
```

### Script Execution Flow (`src/train.py`):
1. **Data Ingestion:** Reads the processed Parquet dataset (`data/processed/land_acquisition_3m.parquet` / `test_dataset.parquet`).
2. **Feature Separation:** Isolates input features from target (`will_be_delayed`).
3. **Leakage Scrubbing:** Drops `project_id`, `snapshot_date`, `state`, `district`, `will_be_delayed`, `additional_delay_days`, and `delay_stage`.
4. **Categorical Encoding:** One-hot encodes object/string columns (`project_type`, `land_type`, `priority`, `current_stage`).
5. **Project Group Split:** Executes `GroupShuffleSplit(n_splits=1, test_size=0.20, random_state=42)` on `project_id` to guarantee zero cross-split project leakage.
6. **Class Imbalance Balancing:** Computes `scale_pos_weight = negative_rows / positive_rows`.
7. **GPU Model Training:** Trains `XGBClassifier` using `tree_method="hist"` and `device="cuda"`.
8. **Test Set Evaluation:** Computes Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 Score, ROC-AUC, and Confusion Matrix against held-out projects.
9. **Artifact Export:** Persists the trained model to `models/baseline_model.json` and the 76-feature name sequence to `models/feature_names.joblib`.

# 🛡️ Prototype Limitations & Governance Notice

Bhoomi Sakha is developed as a prototype decision-support tool for the Smart India Hackathon:

1. **Risk Estimation, Not Determination:** Predictions are mathematical probability estimates based on statistical correlation in historical patterns. They do not constitute deterministic forecasts.
2. **Contribution Signals vs. Causation:** Feature contributions indicate which variables influenced the model's output for a given snapshot. They are **not causal explanations** and do not prove that altering an isolated variable will eliminate project delay.
3. **Synthetic Data Calibration:** The current baseline model is trained on a 3,000,000-snapshot synthetic dataset. Performance figures reflect synthetic evaluation and will differ when calibrated against specific state-level revenue and land records.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop Governance:** Bhoomi Sakha is strictly a **decision-support cockpit**. It does not perform autonomous statutory actions, approve or cancel acquisition proceedings, issue legal determinations, or override the statutory authority of District Collectors or CALA officers under the RFCTLARR Act 2013.
5. **Production Deployment Requirements:** Real-world government deployment would require validation against official gazette notifications, continuous model-drift monitoring, role-based institutional auditing, and formal integration with state revenue and e-Governance portals.
